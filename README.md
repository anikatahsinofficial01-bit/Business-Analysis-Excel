# 📊 Business Analysis Dashboards (Excel, PowerBI)

This repository showcases a collection of **interactive Excel dashboards** designed for comprehensive business performance analysis.  
Each dashboard leverages Excel’s advanced tools — including **pivot tables, Power Query, slicers, and conditional formatting** — to transform raw data into insightful visual reports that support **data-driven decision-making**.

### 📊 2. HR Analytics Dashboard

<img src="https://github.com/anikatahsinofficial01-bit/Business-Analysis-Excel/blob/main/HR%20Analytics%20Dashboard.png" width="800">

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an **HR Analytics Dashboard** developed to analyze workforce trends, employee retention, salary distribution, and job satisfaction. The dashboard provides insights that help HR departments make **data-driven decisions for improving employee experience and organizational performance.**

---

# 📈 Dashboard Metrics

* Total Salary Paid: **10M**
* Total Employees: **1470**
* Current Employees: **1233**
* Terminated Employees: **237**
* Termination Rate: **19.22%**

---

# 🔍 Key Insights

## 👥 1. Workforce Composition

Most employees hold a **Bachelor’s degree**, followed by **Master’s degrees**. Employees with Doctorates represent a very small portion of the workforce.

## ⚖️ 2. Gender Distribution

The workforce consists of approximately:

* **59% Male employees**
* **41% Female employees**

This suggests moderate gender imbalance.

## 🚪 3. Employee Termination

The company has a **termination rate of 19.22%**, meaning nearly **one in five employees leaves the organization**.

Monthly trends indicate higher termination during:

* **August**
* **October**

## 💰 4. Salary Distribution

Salary expenditure is highest among employees with **5–10 years of experience**, indicating salary growth tied to tenure.

## 😊 5. Job Satisfaction

Most employees report **High or Very High satisfaction**, but a notable number still report **Medium or Low satisfaction**, which may lead to future attrition.

## ⚖️ 6. Work-Life Balance

The majority of employees rate their work-life balance as **Better**, but a small group experiences **Poor balance**, which could impact productivity and retention.

---

# ⚠️ Problems Identified

1. High employee termination rate (19.22%)
2. Gender imbalance in workforce
3. Termination spikes in certain months
4. Presence of employees with low job satisfaction
5. Salary concentration among specific tenure groups
6. Some employees reporting poor work-life balance

---

# 💡 Recommendations

## 🔄 Improve Employee Retention

* Implement career development programs
* Introduce employee engagement initiatives

## 🌍 Strengthen Diversity Hiring

* Promote inclusive recruitment strategies
* Build diversity hiring partnerships

## 📉 Investigate Turnover Peaks

* Analyze exit interview data

## 🤝 Enhance Job Satisfaction

* Conduct regular employee surveys
* Improve leadership support and company culture

## 💵 Review Compensation Structure

* Ensure fair salary progression across tenure levels

## 🧘 Improve Work-Life Balance

* Introduce flexible work policies

### ☎️ 3. Sales Call Center Dashboard
<img src="https://github.com/anikatahsinofficial01-bit/Business-Analysis-Excel/blob/main/Sales%20Call%20Center%20Dashboard.jpg" width="800">

**Purpose:**  
Evaluates the performance of sales representatives based on communication efficiency and deal closures.  

**Highlights:**  
- KPIs for Total Calls, Calls Reached, Deals Closed, and Total Deal Value

- **Microsoft Excel:** Pivot Tables, Charts, Power Query, Conditional Formatting  
- **Data Visualization:** Dynamic KPI indicators, interactive filters, and geographic maps  
- **Analytical Focus:** Trend identification, performance comparison, and decision support  

## 🧠 Insights Delivered
These dashboards enable organizations to:
- Track key business KPIs at a glance  
- Identify performance trends and growth opportunities  
- Visualize regional and category-wise performance metrics  
- Make informed business decisions using visual analytics
